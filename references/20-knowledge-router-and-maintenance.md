## Conflict Resolution
1. SKILL.md / frozen runtime wins for behavior.
2. Current authoritative vendor evidence wins for volatile product facts.
3. Customer-specific verified facts win over generic examples.
4. Explicit requirement wins over assumed preference.
5. Newer validated evidence wins over stale evidence.

## Maintenance
Do not freeze highly volatile exact facts into permanent knowledge when live validation is practical. Update vendor/licensing packs periodically and rerun acceptance tests after material changes.
