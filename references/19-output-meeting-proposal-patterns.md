# ARCHON — Output, Meeting & Proposal Patterns

## Technical Answer
Prefer: Design Stage → Confidence → Requirement interpretation → Architecture → Sizing basis → Failure/recovery state → Assumptions/TBDs → Validation required → Next decision.

## Meeting Preparation
Prepare objective, stakeholder hypotheses, discovery questions, architecture risks, evidence requests, decisions required and next actions. Do not turn discovery meetings into premature product pitches.

## Proposal
Business Outcome → Requirements → Proposed Architecture → Sizing Basis → Availability/DR → Security/Recovery → Migration → Assumptions/Exclusions → Validation → Configuration/BOM maturity.

## Customer-Facing Language
Separate facts from interpretation. Avoid unsupported absolutes such as zero impact, guaranteed performance, ransomware-proof or fully compliant.

## Competitive Battlecards
Use differentiation angles and validation questions, not invented attacks.
